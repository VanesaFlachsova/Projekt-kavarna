Interesting Facts

Lake Jasna

Our café is located near Lake Jasna, one of Slovenia's most beautiful alpine lakes surrounded by the Julian Alps.

Meet Our Stingray

Inside our café, you can marvel at the snow leopard stingray, a breathtakingly beautiful fish celebrated for its striking, roselle-like spots and effortless elegance as it glides through the water.
