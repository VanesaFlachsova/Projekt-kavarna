Our History

Cafe with Nessie was founded in 2015 on the shores of the beautiful Lake Jasna in Slovenia. The idea behind the café was simple: to create a peaceful place where visitors could enjoy quality coffee, homemade desserts, and the stunning natural scenery of the Julian Alps.

What began as a small family-owned café soon became a popular destination for travelers, hikers, cyclists, and local residents. Over the years, Cafe with Nessie has built a reputation for its welcoming atmosphere, traditional recipes, and commitment to using fresh local ingredients.

As the number of visitors to Lake Jasna increased, the café expanded its menu and seating area while maintaining the same friendly service and relaxed environment that guests had come to appreciate. The café became a popular stop for people exploring the surrounding mountains and lakes, offering a comfortable place to rest and enjoy refreshments.

Today, Cafe with Nessie welcomes visitors from around the world. Guests can enjoy a variety of coffees, teas, homemade desserts, and seasonal specialties while taking in the views of one of Slovenia's most picturesque locations.

Why "Nessie"?

The café is owned by Vanessa Lestan, who is known to friends, staff, and many regular customers by the nickname "Nessie." The nickname originated from her first name and has stayed with her for many years. Even today, many guests know her simply as Nessie, and visitors may notice that her staff name tag proudly carries the nickname.

When the café was founded, the nickname seemed like the perfect choice for its name. Combined with the local stories surrounding Lake Jasna, it gave the café a unique identity and inspired the name Cafe with Nessie.

The Legend of Lake Jasna

According to local folklore, a mysterious creature has lived beneath the waters of Lake Jasna for centuries. Stories of the creature have been passed down through generations by fishermen, shepherds, and travelers visiting the lake.

The legend describes a gentle guardian of the lake that appears only during quiet mornings or misty evenings. Witnesses claimed to have seen unusual ripples crossing the water without any wind or a dark shape moving just below the surface.

One of the oldest stories tells of a shepherd who became lost during a storm in the surrounding mountains. As he searched for a safe path home, he noticed a strange figure moving through the lake. The creature slowly traveled along the shoreline, and the shepherd followed it until he reached safety. By the time he looked back, it had disappeared.

Whether the legend is true remains unknown. However, the story continues to be one of the most popular tales connected to Lake Jasna and has inspired countless visitors to watch the water carefully in hopes of spotting the lake's mysterious guardian.
